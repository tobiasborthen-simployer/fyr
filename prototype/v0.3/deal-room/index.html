The questions we get asked most.

How long until we're using it?

We agree the timeline with your onboarding specialist at kick-off. It depends on how quickly data and approvals come back from your side. Absence and time first, then the rest in your order. Guided onboarding is included, so you're not setting it up alone.

Will our managers use it?

They approve from their phone or in Teams. That was the whole design brief. It's where most systems fall short, which is why the customers above had the same problem you do. Ask them about it.

What happens to the data in our payroll system?

We move it over. Mapping happens in weeks 2 and 3, and you approve it before anything goes live. Payroll stays where it is, and the integration keeps both sides up to date.

Could we start on Essential and move up later?

Yes. Moving to Complete keeps your data and setup, and adds the extra parts on top. For you, it would mean going without Equal Pay, surveys, and goals in the meantime, and Expert would be the knowledge bank only. With your reporting deadline, that's the part I'd think hard about.

Do we have to take everything at once?

No. You start with absence and time, then add the rest in the order on the implementation plan. Nothing forces the pace.

Who do we talk to with an HR question?

Questions on the rules go to Sia, and you get the answer with the source. For input on your situation, you call or write to an HR expert employed by Simployer, someone who's seen cases like yours. It's included in Complete.

Will pay mapping be done before our reporting deadline?

We set the order so it's done before your deadline, and we confirm that at kick-off. If the deadline moves, tell me and we'll move it up the list.
